# Mitra Character Contract

This document serves as the absolute source of truth for the visual identity and structural traits of the Mitra companion in Sthira. 

**IMPORTANT**: The experimental "Chibi" and pixel-art character directions are officially retired. Mitra is NOT a Chibi character. The master asset (`assets/mitra/canonical/mitra_master.png`) is the strict visual lock.

- **MITRA_MASTER**: The core identity source.
- **MITRA_IDEAL**: The canonical everyday reference pose. Future animation states will explicitly develop from this stance.

## 1. Visual Specification
- **Subject:** Young/Baby Elephant Companion.
- **Color Palette:** Warm grey body tone, complemented by large soft peach inner ears.
- **Identifying Features:** 
  - Large, highly expressive intelligent eyes.
  - Small, delicate ivory tusks.
  - A natural, tapered expressive trunk without exaggerated cartoon distortion.
  - A distinctive tail.
- **Accessories:** A softly draped peach/plum neckerchief.
- **Aesthetic Style:** Premium hand-painted 2D illustration. Smooth shading with naturalistic forms, entirely distinct from generic vector mascots or pixel-art.

## 2. Personality Profile
- Confident, slightly goofy, and inherently supportive.
- Acts as a benevolent, playful "older-brother" figure rather than a simple pet or collectible doll.

## 3. Engineering Constraints
- The master asset must **NOT** be regenerated, stylized, simplified, or recolored.
- Future dynamic poses (once approved for Phase 2+) must strictly adhere to the proportions, shading quality, and character details shown in the `mitra_master.png` asset.
- The canonical master asset pipeline guarantees uniform handling of alpha channels and bounding boxes without introducing destructive chromatic halos.
